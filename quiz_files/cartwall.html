Settings

Keys: Esc stop all · Space fade all · PgUp/PgDn or F1–F12 change page.
Loading: click an empty cart, drag WAVs onto a cart (several fill onwards from it), or use Load WAVs. Right-click any cart to edit it. Pages: + adds a page, double-click a tab to rename it, and the ✕ on a tab removes that page.
Normalise: each WAV's loudness is measured once when it's loaded (LUFS, the broadcast standard), and carts are turned up or down to the target level so they all sound about as loud as each other. A cart's own Gain is added on top. Quiet tracks are only boosted as far as their peaks allow, up to +12 dB.
Carts and their audio are saved in this browser and reloaded automatically next time.
